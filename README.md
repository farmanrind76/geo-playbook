# GEO Playbook

A practical framework for getting a brand cited in AI answers: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Gemini, Claude and Copilot.

Written by [Farman Rind](https://farmanrind.com), SEO and GEO specialist. Real results and examples: [case studies](https://farmanrind.com/case-study/).

> Figures below come from published industry studies and my working notes. Treat them as directional and verify before quoting them in a client deck.

## 1. Why GEO is different from SEO

- **Passage-level retrieval.** AI engines pull roughly 130 to 260 word chunks, not whole pages. Each passage has to stand on its own.
- **Query fan-out.** One user question expands into many sub-queries before retrieval. You need to answer the sub-questions, not just the head term.
- **Probabilistic answers.** Only about 30% of brands stay visible across back-to-back responses to the same prompt.
- **Off-site signals dominate.** Roughly 85% of citations come from third-party sources, not the brand's own site.
- **Citation is not traffic.** Very few users click a link inside an AI summary, so measure visibility and brand impact, not just sessions.
- **The unit of competition changed.** It is the brand as an entity in the AI corpus, not the individual page.

## 2. How each AI surface behaves

| Surface | What matters |
|---|---|
| Perplexity | Hybrid keyword plus dense retrieval at section level. Returns fewer sources rather than weak ones. Citations convert well. |
| ChatGPT | Default answers come from training data. Search mode queries Bing, so Bing indexing matters. Check that OpenAI bots are not blocked in robots.txt. |
| Google AI Overviews / AI Mode | Query fan-out plus passage ranking. Most cited URLs already rank in the top 10 organically. |
| Claude | Native chat uses the training corpus. Web research routes through Brave Search, and rewards technical depth and multi-source agreement. |
| Gemini | Shares the AI Mode architecture and leans on the Google Knowledge Graph, so a clean Wikidata entity helps. |
| Copilot | Shares the Bing and OpenAI backend. Leans toward business publications. |

Only a small share of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity, so optimize for each surface as well as for the universal basics.

## 3. On-site checklist

- [ ] Write self-contained passages of about 130 to 260 words and lead with the answer
- [ ] Show named authors with Author schema and sameAs links (E-E-A-T)
- [ ] Build topic clusters: one pillar page plus 15 to 30 supporting pages, linked with descriptive anchors
- [ ] Add schema: FAQPage where relevant, plus 3 or more relevant types per key page
- [ ] Write for query fan-out: cover the sub-questions a buyer would ask
- [ ] Publish listicles and comparison pages, a format that is heavily cited
- [ ] Publish original data with a clear methodology section
- [ ] Optimize images and video: descriptive alt text, ImageObject and VideoObject schema, full transcripts
- [ ] Keep content fresh with a visible last-updated date
- [ ] Technical base: allow GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended and CCBot; submit sitemaps; enable IndexNow; avoid heavy client-side JavaScript

Note: llms.txt has no measured link to citation rates. It is a cheap extra, not a real lever.

## 4. Off-site checklist (where most of the work is)

- [ ] Reddit: warm up the account, follow the 90/10 rule, always disclose affiliation, never astroturf
- [ ] Review platforms (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot): claim and fully complete every profile
- [ ] Wikipedia and Wikidata: once the brand is notable, keep the Wikidata entity accurate
- [ ] Earned media and digital PR: named-expert quotes and case studies work best
- [ ] YouTube: substantive tutorials with edited transcripts
- [ ] LinkedIn: treat it as a publication platform, not just a profile
- [ ] Forums and communities: Stack Exchange, Quora, Hacker News, vertical forums
- [ ] Google Business Profile for location-based intent: complete profile, review replies, regular posts

## 5. Measurement

1. **Visibility:** citation rate and share of voice across a fixed prompt set
2. **Source quality:** which third-party sources get cited, versus the 85% off-site benchmark
3. **Traffic and pipeline:** GA4 AI referrals and the Bing Webmaster AI Performance report
4. **Brand and entity:** what AI says about the brand, with a fact inventory, weekly monitoring and a correction process

## 6. Maturity phases

| Phase | State | Exit trigger |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Baseline unknown | Run the first prompt audit |
| 1 | Foundation in place, still invisible | 10%+ citation rate on 2+ surfaces |
| 2 | Sporadic citations | 25 to 30%+ citation rate and 3+ off-site channels |
| 3 | Consistent, not the default | First-named brand in 40%+ of top prompts |
| 4 | Category leader | Maintain |
